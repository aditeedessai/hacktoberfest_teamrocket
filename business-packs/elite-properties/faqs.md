## Are utilities included in the rent?

## Can I schedule an in-person property viewing?
Yes. Prospective clients may request viewing appointments on Wednesdays and Saturdays between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM. We require at least 24 hours advance notice and government-issued photo identification upon arrival.

## Can the AI assistant confirm my viewing appointment or approve my application?
No. The assistant can record your preferred viewing date and property details, but it cannot confirm appointments, approve leases, or negotiate prices. A licensed Elite Properties agent will review your request and contact you to confirm the appointment.
