# Sweet Home Kitchen — Frequently Asked Questions

## What cakes do you bake and what are their prices?
We bake three signature celebration cakes:
- **Belgian Chocolate Truffle Cake:** 1 kg ($35.00) | 2 kg ($65.00)
- **Classic Vanilla Bean Cake:** 1 kg ($30.00) | 2 kg ($55.00)
- **Red Velvet Deluxe Cake:** 1 kg ($38.00) | 2 kg ($70.00)

## What are your store hours and location?
We are located at 42 Maple Street, Downtown Crossing, Springfield.
- Tuesday – Saturday: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- Sunday: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
- Monday: Closed

## Do you offer local delivery?
Yes. We deliver within a 5-mile radius from 42 Maple Street.
- Delivery fee: Flat $5.00, or complimentary on orders exceeding $50.00.
- Delivery hours: Tuesday – Saturday from 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Sunday from 10:00 AM to 2:30 PM (before store closes at 3:00 PM).

## How much advance notice is required for an order?
- Standard celebration cakes require at least 24 hours advance notice.
- Gluten-friendly and eggless cakes require at least 48 hours advance notice.
- Daily sourdough breads and croissant boxes are available for same-day walk-in purchase while batch stocks last.

## Do you offer gluten-friendly or eggless cakes?
- **Gluten-friendly:** Available for 1 kg Chocolate Truffle and Vanilla Bean cakes (+$5.00, requires 48 hours advance notice).
- **Eggless:** Available for Vanilla Bean cakes in 1 kg and 2 kg sizes at no extra charge (requires 48 hours advance notice).
- **Allergen Notice:** All items are baked in a kitchen that handles wheat flour, dairy, eggs, and tree nuts. We cannot guarantee zero trace cross-contamination. Any customer reporting an airborne or life-threatening allergy must be escalated to human staff.

## Can I order a multi-tier wedding cake or custom fondant sculpture?
We do not quote or confirm complex custom wedding cakes (over 2 tiers) or sculpted fondant figurines via automated chat. These require an in-person consultation with our head pastry chef. The assistant will escalate your request to our team.

## What is your cancellation and refund policy?
- **Policy:** Orders canceled at least 24 hours before the scheduled pickup or delivery time are eligible for a 100% store credit or full refund. Same-day cancellations cannot be refunded because our baked goods are prepared to order.
- **Action Rule:** The assistant can explain this policy to customers, but any active cancellation, refund request, or payment dispute must be escalated to a human staff member to process.
