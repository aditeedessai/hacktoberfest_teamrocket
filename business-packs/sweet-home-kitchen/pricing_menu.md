# Sweet Home Kitchen — Menu & Pricing

## Handcrafted Celebration Cakes
- **Belgian Chocolate Truffle Cake:**
  - Rich 54% Belgian dark chocolate ganache, moist chocolate sponge, cocoa nibs.
  - 1 kg (serves 6–8): $35.00
  - 2 kg (serves 12–16): $65.00
  - Dietary options: Gluten-friendly sponge available (1 kg only, +$5.00, requires 48 hours notice).
- **Classic Vanilla Bean Cake:**
  - Madagascar bourbon vanilla bean sponge layered with whipped mascarpone frosting.
  - 1 kg (serves 6–8): $30.00
  - 2 kg (serves 12–16): $55.00
  - Dietary options: Eggless version available upon request (no extra charge, requires 48 hours notice); Gluten-friendly sponge available (1 kg only, +$5.00, requires 48 hours notice).
- **Red Velvet Deluxe Cake:**
  - Traditional buttermilk cocoa sponge with Madagascar cream cheese swirl frosting.
  - 1 kg (serves 6–8): $38.00
  - 2 kg (serves 12–16): $70.00

## Artisanal Breads & Morning Pastries
- **Classic Country Sourdough Loaf:** 800g naturally fermented artisan loaf: $8.00
- **Butter Croissant Box (Pack of 6):** Flaky French-style laminated all-butter croissants: $18.00
- **Pain au Chocolat (Pack of 4):** Laminated pastry filled with dark chocolate batons: $15.00

## Permitted Add-ons & Customizations
- **Custom Plaque Inscription:** Hand-piped chocolate plaque (e.g., "Happy Birthday Sarah"): Free on request with any celebration cake.
- **Birthday Sparkler Candle Set:** Pack of gold sparkler candles: $3.50
- **Gluten-Friendly Sponge Option:** Available for 1 kg Chocolate Truffle and Vanilla Bean cakes only (+$5.00, requires 48 hours notice).
- **Eggless Sponge Option:** Available for 1 kg and 2 kg Vanilla Bean cakes only (no extra charge, requires 48 hours notice).

## Unsupported Customizations (Require Chef Consultation)
- Custom multi-tier wedding cakes (over 2 tiers).
- Fondant sculpted figurines and complex 3D novelty designs.
- Large event catering (>50 servings).
- Inquiries for unsupported customizations must be escalated to the head pastry chef.

## Required Lead Times
- Standard Celebration Cakes (1 kg & 2 kg): Minimum 24 hours advance notice.
- Gluten-Friendly or Eggless Custom Cakes: Minimum 48 hours advance notice.
- Sourdough Bread & Pastry Boxes: Available for same-day walk-in purchase while daily batch stocks last.
