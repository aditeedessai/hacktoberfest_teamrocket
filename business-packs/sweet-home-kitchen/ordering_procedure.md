# Sweet Home Kitchen — Ordering Procedure

## Information to collect
When a customer wants to place an order, collect:
1. Customer name
2. Contact number
3. Cake or product requested
4. Quantity
5. Required date
6. Preferred pickup or delivery
7. Additional instructions

## Procedure
- Ask politely for any missing information.
- Do not invent prices or confirm availability.
- Do not claim an order has been placed.
- Do not claim payment has been received.
- Once the required details are collected, escalate to a human team member for confirmation.

## Escalation cases
Escalate custom designs, allergies, refunds, complaints, urgent orders, and requests involving unconfigured details.
